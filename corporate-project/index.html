How We Simplified Employee Travel Management with AI for GMR Corporate users

Worked on simplifying the Travel Request (TR) journey and designing an AI-assisted Travel Expense (TE) filing experience through UX research, user flows, wireframes, high-fidelity designs, and cross-functional collaboration.

Shayan Alam

UI UX Designer

PRODUCT CONTEXT

Hoi - Corporate Travel

Hoi partnered with GMR to transform their employee travel operations from a paper-based workflow into a digital-first experience. We designed a Corporate Travel module that enabled employees to manage Travel Requests and Expense Claims seamlessly, while leveraging AI to automate expense filing and reduce manual effort.

Project Overview

Outline

This project highlights how we uncovered usability challenges in corporate travel management and redesigned the Travel Request (TR) journey while incorporating AI powered Travel Expense (TE) filing. The solution simplifies complex workflows, automates expense reporting, and improves the overall employee experience.

Problem

  1. Corporate travelers currently face a complex, time-consuming Travel Request (TR) creation process, with multiple categories, fields, and decisions presented at once. This increases cognitive load, slows task completion, and raises the likelihood of errors or incomplete submissions. Returning users enter the same travel information, creating unnecessary effort and frustration.

  2. Corporate employees currently spend 30+ minutes filing Travel Expenses (TE) because the process is manual, fragmented, and error-prone. Users repeatedly enter expense details, attach receipts, and verify policy compliance, which causes submission delays, inaccurate data, and frequent policy violations. This increases administrative effort for employees and approvers, reduces productivity, and creates frustration throughout the expense reporting journey.

Gathering Insights

Designing the mobile version of a Corporate Travel system (including TR – Travel Request and TE – Travel Expense) means prioritizing essential features, simplified UI, and fast access, since users are likely on the move. Here’s a focused approach i took while designing for the same:

Observation

The TR creation process is complex, requiring multiple clicks and lacking clear navigation. Similarly, the TE filing process is complicated, with manual expense entry making it time-consuming and inefficient.

True Problem

The form-filling process is complex and time-consuming, making it difficult for users to complete and verify their profiles efficiently. This creates problem in Raising of TR and Settling TE.

Scope of the feature

Redesign the Corporate Travel experience by simplifying the Travel Request (TR) creation journey with a guided, intuitive flow while leveraging AI to automate Travel Expense (TE) filing. The solution aims to reduce manual effort, eliminate repetitive tasks, improve accuracy and policy compliance, and enable employees to complete travel requests and expense submissions faster with a seamless end-to-end experience.

Desired Outcome

Create a faster, smarter, and more intuitive corporate travel experience by simplifying Travel Request creation and automating Travel Expense filing with AI. The solution aims to reduce manual effort, shorten completion time, improve accuracy and policy compliance, and deliver a seamless end-to-end experience for employees and approvers.

The Solution - (For Problem Statement 2)

Our proposed solution uses AI to auto fill expense details from receipts and guides users through a simplified TE filing flow. Built-in policy validation reduces errors, cuts filing time, and improves accuracy.

Old Flow

In the old flow, corporate employees spent 30+ minutes filing Travel Expenses (TE) due to a manual, fragmented, and error-prone process. Entering every expense detail was time-consuming and frustrating.

The user had to fill each lengthy form i.e Ticket Expense, Accommodation, Per Diem, Conveyance, Miscellaneous, Forex Advance and come back to the summary page

AI + Travel Expense

To address our users' biggest pain point, we introduced Joey—Hoi AI's intelligent expense filing assistant. Joey automatically extracts data from receipts, invoices, and PDFs to pre-fill Travel Expense (TE) forms in real time, eliminating manual data entry. Powered by AI and informed by competitor research, it reduces filing time from 30+ minutes to under 10 minutes while improving accuracy and minimizing policy violations.

To simplify the experience, we broke the AI Expense Filing flow into five easy-to-follow sections, making the process more structured, intuitive, and user-friendly.
