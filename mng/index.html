Feature Improvement - Meet & Greet

Meet & Greet service is a premium airport assistance service offered by Hoi app, designed to make the airport journey faster, smoother, and stress-free. In other words Meet & Greet is a concierge service where an airport executive assists passengers through airport procedures from entry to boarding or arrival to exit.

  1. How we Approached the Problem?

The current Meet & Greet Hoi flow forces users to make multiple manual selections, including terminal, flight, and date, which adds unnecessary steps, increases effort, and raises drop-off rates. It also creates friction when users try to edit the number of passengers from the cart, making the booking experience less seamless and efficient.

  1. What’s wrong with the current flow

Users are required to select the airport terminal and location before entering their flight number. This manual process increases the likelihood of errors, as the selected terminal may not match the entered flight, resulting in booking friction and validation failures.

The Solution

Increase Purchase Confidence

Insight 1

Google Analytics showed high drop-off after users searched by flight number + date, especially when terminal mismatches dumped them into error states.

Solution:

Removed flight number from the booking journey entirely and moved flight details to post-booking confirmation.

• Applied error prevention by cutting the dependency on flight search, so terminal mismatch failures can't happen in the booking path

• Applied progressive disclosure by collecting operational details only after booking intent is set, not before

Insight 2

Both flight-first and package-first journeys showed high abandonment, pointing to the flight lookup itself as the friction.

Solution:

Let users commit to the service before entering operational details, removing the lookup as an upfront gate.

• Applied Hick's Law by cutting early decisions, so users reach commitment faster

• Reduced friction at the top of the funnel by not asking for operational input before the user has chosen to book

Insight 3

Users consistently explored "Compare All Plans" before deciding, but the comparison page dead-ended with no way to continue booking.

Solution:

Added a "Book Now" CTA inside the Compare All Plans experience, turning the comparison page into a conversion point.

• Applied recognition over recall by letting users compare plans and continue without losing their place

• Converted an information-only page into a booking surface, meeting users where the decision is actually made

Insight 4

Users frequently returned from the cart to change guest count, causing backtracking and drop-off.

Solution:

Removed guest selection from the flow and moved "Add Guest" into the cart, with pricing that recalculates from the passenger details entered.

• Applied journey continuity by placing edits where users naturally expect them, eliminating the round-trip

• Removed upfront complexity by calculating price dynamically from adult/child/infant details instead of asking for guest count early

Cross Sell Focus

Clarity session recordings revealed that users were open to exploring other services within the app, especially after completing a booking, indicating these were high-intent moments for service discovery.

Solution:

Introduced contextual cross-selling across key touch points, in the Meet & Greet confirmation page, surfacing relevant services based on the user's

current journey.

• Applied contextual relevance by recommending services when they were most useful, making discovery feel timely rather than promotional.

• Increased service discoverability and cross-feature adoption by turning high-intent moments into natural entry points for related airport experiences
